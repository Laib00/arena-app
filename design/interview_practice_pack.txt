2. SETTINGS BY STUDENT GRADE
================================================================================

--- SHARED (all grades can use) ---
Phone / Video Screen
  Short scheduled call. Limited time. Keep questions focused; cut long answers.

In-Person Interview
  Face-to-face in an office, school room, or meeting space. Fuller answers OK.
  Notice composure; stay professional.

--- HIGH SCHOOL / JC only (or primarily) ---
School / Career Counsellor Style
  Feels supportive but still evaluative. Often for CCA, leadership, or
  school-arranged opportunities. Language is clearer; less corporate jargon.

Admissions / Scholarship Panel
  Formal panel for school, JC, university admission, or scholarship.
  Refer to "we" / the committee. Academic motivation and character matter.

Holiday Internship Chat
  Informal-but-real chat with a supervisor or HR for a short internship.
  Expect enthusiasm, reliability, and basic responsibility — not deep expertise.

--- POLYTECHNIC ---
Industrial Attachment Interview
  Company interviewing for attachment / internship tied to diploma.
  Care about attitude, safety/reliability, and willingness to learn on-site.

Campus / Career Fair Screen
  Noisy, time-boxed fair conversation. Short answers. Clear interest in the
  company wins over long stories.

--- UNIVERSITY / FRESH GRAD ---
Hiring Manager Round
  Proper manager interview for internship or junior role. Expect STAR examples
  from projects, CCA, internships, or part-time work.

Assessment / Panel Round
  Panel or assessment-style interview. Multiple angles; stay harder to please
  than a single friendly HR screen.

Final / Decision Round
  Late-stage. Dig into judgment, motivation, and team fit. Less "getting to
  know you," more "would we trust you."

3. AIMS BY STUDENT GRADE
================================================================================
Phrase for AI: "Your goal is to…"

--- HIGH SCHOOL / JC ---
Introduce Yourself Clearly
  see if they can introduce who they are, what they study, and what they want
  — simply and confidently (not a corporate elevator pitch)

Why This Opportunity
  assess motivation for this part-time job / CCA role / school / scholarship
  — specific reasons, not "I want to gain experience" only

Responsibility & Reliability
  probe whether they show up on time, handle basic responsibility, and can
  give a real example of being dependable (CCA, group project, family duty)

Teamwork / Group Project
  assess how they work with classmates or CCA teammates — one concrete example

Handling Pressure / Mistakes
  ask about a mistake, bad grade moment, or conflict — look for honesty and
  learning, not perfection

Admissions / Scholarship Motivation
  assess academic interest, goals, and character for school or scholarship
  panels (curiosity > memorised slogans)

Questions for Us
  see if they can ask one thoughtful question about the role, school, or team

--- POLYTECHNIC ---
Tell Me About Yourself (Diploma Context)
  connect their diploma course and projects to this attachment / junior role

Why This Company / Attachment
  check basic research and genuine interest in the work environment

Course Project / Skills Example
  dig into one school project or module — what they personally did

Workplace Attitude
  reliability, feedback to feedback, safety/professional behaviour on site

Customer / Team Situation
  simple behavioral: difficult classmate, customer, or group conflict

Questions for Us
  end-of-interview questions that show curiosity about real work

--- UNIVERSITY / FRESH GRAD ---
Tell Me About Yourself / Fit
  clear intro linking studies, projects, internships to this role

Behavioral (STAR)
  competency questions with specific Situation–Task–Action–Result

Motivation / Why This Role
  researched, realistic reasons for role and company

Technical / Course Depth (light)
  explain a project or concept from their field without buzzword fog

Weakness, Failure & Conflict
  real self-awareness under harder questions

Case / Mini Scenario
  short workplace or campus scenario; judge structured thinking

Closing & Questions for Us
  professional close and thoughtful questions

================================================================================
(Optional chips. Same shape: id, label, promptHint.
Only show challenges allowed for that studentGrade.)

--- ALL GRADES (wording stays age-appropriate in prompt) ---
rambling_answer          Candidate rambles / no structure
silent_or_short          Very short / thin answers
why_this_opportunity     Vague motivation ("gain experience")
nervous_recovery         Nerves — can they recover after a shaky start?
no_questions_for_us      Says "no questions" at the end
time_pressure            Strict time box

--- HIGH SCHOOL / JC ---
cca_or_project_probe     Deep dive on one CCA / school project (their role)
responsibility_check     Punctuality / reliability example
scholarship_values       Character / values question (integrity, service)
parents_expectation      (Careful) Goals vs family pressure — stay sensitive,
                         never mock family; look for clarity of own goals
group_conflict_school    Group project freeloader / disagreement

--- POLYTECHNIC ---
attachment_readiness     Are they ready for real workplace norms?
module_deep_dive         Explain a diploma module/project in plain language
customer_facing_pressure Simple difficult customer / stakeholder scenario
feedback_openness        How they take correction from supervisor/lecturer

--- UNIVERSITY / FRESH GRAD ---
behavioral_probe         Hard STAR follow-ups
failure_question         Real failure / weakness (no "perfectionist")
technical_deep_dive      Buzzword challenge on their field
competing_candidates     Why you vs other grads
cv_gap_or_switch         Gap, short stint, or course vs role mismatch
salary_expectation       Only for fresh_grad / final-year uni (optional);
                         keep light — ranges, not hard negotiation theatre
skeptical_interviewer    Polite doubt on ownership claims
curveball_question       Unusual but professional question

DO NOT show high schoolers:
  salary_expectation (heavy), technical_deep_dive (senior), panel_disagreement
  as corporate politics, "5 years experience" framing, exclusive senior roles
